# 05 — UTR Number in Refund Emails

**Type:** Product Operations Project  
**Stack:** Return Prime · Razorpay · Webhooks  
**Severity:** High — Customer Trust & Support Load  
**Status:** ✅ Resolved

---

## Problem Statement

Customers receiving refund confirmation emails had no way to track their refund — the UTR (Unique Transaction Reference) number was missing. This happened because Return Prime triggered refund emails at the moment of **refund initiation**, but UTR numbers are only generated asynchronously by Razorpay **after** the actual payment processing is complete.

The result: thousands of refund emails sent with incomplete information, leading to a flood of "where is my UTR?" support queries.

---

## Why UTR Matters

The UTR number is the banking reference that allows customers to:
- Confirm a refund has been processed by their bank
- Track refund status with their bank if it's delayed
- Have documented proof of refund for disputes

Without it, customers have no way to verify the refund independently.

---

## Root Cause: Timing Mismatch

### Razorpay Refund Lifecycle

```
Refund Initiated (instant)
    ↓
Razorpay Processes Refund (asynchronous)
    ↓
UTR Generated by Banking Layer (delayed — minutes to hours)
    ↓
Webhook Fired by Razorpay (confirms UTR)
```

### Return Prime's Behavior (Before Fix)

```
Refund Initiated
    ↓
Return Prime Immediately Sends Refund Email  ← EMAIL SENT HERE
    ↓
(UTR not yet available)
    ↓
Customer Receives Email with No UTR
    ↓
Customer Contacts Support: "Where is the UTR?"
```

The email trigger was mapped to the **initiation event**, not the **completion event**.

---

## Business Impact (Before Fix)

- High volume of refund-related support queries
- Support team spending significant time manually tracking UTRs and following up
- Customers unable to independently verify refund status
- Reduced confidence in refund process
- Poor post-purchase brand experience

---

## Solution: Webhook-Gated Email Workflow

### Step 1 — Diagnosis & Coordination

Identified the timing mismatch through analysis of the Return Prime + Razorpay workflow. Connected with the Return Prime technical team and clearly explained:
- The UTR lifecycle timing issue
- Customer trust impact
- Rising support query volume
- Need for webhook-based email gating

### Step 2 — Razorpay Webhook Integration

Configured Return Prime to integrate with Razorpay's webhook system:
- Set up secure webhook endpoint
- Configured webhook signature/authentication validation
- Configured Return Prime to listen for `refund.processed` (or equivalent finalized refund event)

This allowed Return Prime to receive the actual refund completion data — including UTR — from Razorpay dynamically.

### Step 3 — UTR Verification & Customer Mapping

Built verification logic:
- Webhook response checked for confirmed UTR number
- UTR matched to correct customer/order record
- Only validated refund records triggered customer email

### Step 4 — Email Trigger Moved to Post-UTR

Refund email workflow updated:

```
BEFORE:  Refund Initiated → Email Sent (no UTR)

AFTER:   Refund Initiated
             ↓
         Razorpay Processes Refund
             ↓
         Webhook Fired → UTR Confirmed
             ↓
         UTR Mapped to Customer/Order
             ↓
         Email Triggered → Customer Receives Verified UTR
```

Also contributed to HTML/CSS improvements in the refund email template for better professional presentation.

```

```

## Key Learnings

- **Event timing matters in integration design.** Triggering on `initiated` vs `completed` produces completely different customer outcomes.
- **Webhooks are the right solution for async data dependencies** — polling would introduce delays and complexity; event-driven is cleaner.
- **Authentication is non-negotiable.** Webhook signature validation prevents spoofed refund confirmations.
- **Support query volume is a product signal.** A surge in "where is my UTR?" queries pointed directly to a workflow gap.

---

## Prevention Recommendations

- [ ] Monitor webhook delivery success rate from Razorpay
- [ ] Add fallback: if UTR webhook not received within 24h, send "refund in progress" interim email
- [ ] Log UTR mapping failures separately for investigation
- [ ] Alert on UTR delivery failure rate exceeding threshold
